## Face validity check

Before finalizing your estimates and AI outputs, ask whether the results
would make sense to someone familiar with the company, industry,
customer, employee role, or process you are studying. Face validity does
not prove your system is correct — it shows your results are not
obviously unrealistic, backwards, or disconnected from the real business
context.

Answer each of the four questions in 1–3 sentences:

1. **Direction.** Does the AI-supported process reduce time where AI
   should help, while keeping (or increasing) human review where
   judgment is still needed?

2. **Magnitude.** Is the size of the effect plausible? A 20% time
   reduction may be reasonable; a 95% reduction needs a strong
   explanation.

3. **Evidence alignment.** Does the AI output, recommendation, ranking,
   forecast, or report match the evidence shown to the user — not just
   sound confident?

4. **Practical constraints.** Does the result respect real-world
   constraints such as approvals, compliance, staffing, customer
   expectations, or data availability?

If any answer is "no" or "I'm not sure," say what you would change or
investigate before relying on the estimate.
