## Common Drink Families

### Lattes
- Base: espresso + steamed milk.
- Customizable: milk type, syrup, temperature (hot or iced).
- Popular: Caffe Latte, Caramel Macchiato, Vanilla Latte, Chai Latte.

### Cold Brew / Iced Coffee
- Cold Brew: steeped cold for 20+ hours; smooth and low-acid.
- Iced Coffee: brewed hot, chilled, served over ice.
- Refreshing, higher caffeine per ounce than lattes.

### Frappuccino
- Blended ice + milk + flavored base.
- Always cold/frozen.
- Not espresso-based unless specified (e.g. Coffee Frappuccino).

### Refreshers
- Fruit-juice base with green coffee extract (mild caffeine).
- Popular dairy-free / vegan option.
- Examples: Strawberry Açaí, Mango Dragonfruit, Passion Tango.

### Teas
- Hot teas: steeped bags.
- Iced teas: shaken or over ice.
- Chai: spiced tea concentrate with milk — can be iced or hot.
- Matcha: green tea powder with milk — can be iced or hot.

## Key Customization Concepts

1. **Size**: Short (8oz) → Tall (12oz) → Grande (16oz) → Venti (20/24oz) → Trenta (30oz cold only).
2. **Milk**: Dairy or plant-based alternatives. Default is 2% for most drinks.
3. **Syrup pumps**: Standard quantity varies by size; customer can request less or more.
4. **Temperature**: Most drinks are available hot or iced on request.
5. **Extra shot**: Customer can add an espresso shot to most espresso drinks.
6. **Decaf**: Espresso can be ordered decaf; brewed coffee decaf may have limited availability.

## Dietary Flags

- **Vegan**: No animal products (no milk, no honey, no whipped cream).
- **Gluten-Free**: No wheat-based ingredients. Cross-contact possible in-store.
- Always direct customers with serious dietary restrictions to the official allergen guide.
  See dietary_allergen_guidance.md for guidance on what to say.
